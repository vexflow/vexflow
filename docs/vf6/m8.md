## Guitar TAB · VF-030

VF5 already draws brush, roll and rasgueado marks. M6 also covers paired staff/TAB, tuning, capo, a grace slide and bend phases. The new work belongs at the level that owns the action. A finger stroke can belong to a chord, a bend to one string, a palm mute to a passage and a golpe to a simultaneous unpitched attack. [Native strokes](../../src/strokes.ts) · [Stroke tests](../../tests/strokes_tests.ts) · [M6 guitar study](m6.md#reviewed-studies)

| Original study | What to write | What the player should be able to recover |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Flamenco rasgueado and golpe | A short compás excerpt with an accented multi-finger rasgueado, a return stroke and a golpe coincident with one stroke. Show the same passage in paired notation and rhythmic TAB. Print a small legend for the chosen finger and arrow convention. | The finger, stroke order, direction across the strings and accent are unambiguous. The golpe adds no beat. Switching the engraving convention preserves the physical stroke. Ask a flamenco guitarist to demonstrate the sequence from the page. |
| Brazilian violão | Start with a bossa nova batida in 2/4. Write thumb bass and syncopated chord attacks in independent voices. Sustain the bass through an offbeat chord, tie an anticipation across a barline, then introduce a deliberately muted attack. Add a contrasting samba accompaniment when a player supplies the reference pattern. | The player can tap the bass and chord rhythms separately, then play them together. The muted attack is distinguishable from a rest and a held chord. The paired staff and TAB agree on strings, attacks and releases. Seven-string choro baixaria is a later study with its own bass-line and tuning requirements. |
| Heavy-metal rhythm and lead | A drop-tuned riff with a palm-muted pedal string under ringing upper notes. Follow it with a tapped hammer-on/pull-off figure and a two-string bend where only one string changes pitch. Force the mute or bend across a system break. | The player can identify the muted strings, fretted and tapped pitches, bend amount, hold and release. A bend never attaches to the neighboring string. Extend to pinch harmonics and whammy-bar dips after reviewing how to distinguish a touched fret, a sounding pitch and a whole-instrument gesture. |

An edition may draw an up-pointing arrow for a physical bass-to-treble downstroke. Store the order of strings and the finger independently of the displayed arrow. Give measured strokes exact offsets inside a parent duration. Allow an explicitly unmeasured roll. For bends, preserve pitch change and musical offset independently of curve width. Test a narrow view where the player needs to see the continuation without a repeated attack. [Published flamenco staff and cifra legend](https://content.alfred.com/catpages/12-0571531024.pdf) · [Nelson Faria, The Brazilian Guitar Book](https://www.shermusic.com/products/brazilian-guitar-book-samba-bossa-nova-and-other-brazilian-styles) · [Guitar Pro effect notation](https://www.guitar-pro.com/docs/gp8/score/note/symbols/effects)

## Contemporary notation · VF-035

- **Feathered beams.** Write accelerating and decelerating groups with an explicit total duration and a shared downbeat in a second part. Include a case with exact authored subdivisions and another with only an overall duration. Test fan direction, clearance, endpoint alignment and a narrow width. A width change must preserve every authored onset. [LilyPond beam examples](https://lilypond.org/doc/v2.26/Documentation/notation/beams.html)
- **Pitch clusters.** Start with bounded clusters on a keyboard staff. Define low and high written pitches, accidental or key-color instructions, duration and stem attachment. Test both stem directions, ledger lines and a neighboring voice. Ask the pianist to identify the intended range and pitches from the printed part.
- **Spatial time and repeat cells.** Put an exact proportional passage beside a boxed, freely repeated cell. Give the cell an entry cue, an allowed time range and an ensemble re-entry point. Check proportional distances within the exact region. At a forced break, either keep a cell intact or apply its declared continuation rule. The reader must be able to locate the cue at both widths. Represent an unknown number of repetitions explicitly. [LilyPond proportional notation](https://lilypond.org/doc/v2.27/Documentation/notation/proportional-notation.html)

## Advanced ensemble layout · VF-020 and VF-034

- **Independent meters.** Extend the existing two bars of 3/4 against three bars of 2/4 through a meter change, a sustained note, a forced system break and a common rehearsal cue. Keep exact ensemble onsets, staff-local bar numbers and local beat groups. Assert that local barlines and beams follow their own staff while equal onsets align. A layout spacer must never appear as a performed rest. [M6 independent-meter study](m6.md#reviewed-studies)
- **Conductor score and extracted parts.** Give a B-flat clarinet a flute cue in the player's written pitch, then add an ossia spanning two measures. Give the part a different system break from the score. Assert sounding-pitch equivalence, cue attribution, rests, continuation and printable page bounds. Use the unedited individual parts as the controls for the next increment. [M5 publication views](m5.md)
- **Two-player condensing.** Use two flute parts with a unison phrase, a split, a solo entrance and a return to unison. Start with an editor choosing the phrase boundaries and player assignments. Then allow automatic merging only in the reviewed cases. Conflicting articulations, rhythms or meters must preserve both readings or fall back to separate staves. Check player labels, source IDs, slurs, rests and extracted parts. Leave full string-section divisi and unrestricted orchestral optimization for a later charter. [Dorico condensing results](https://www.steinberg.help/r/dorico-pro/6.2/en/dorico/topics/page_formatting/page_formatting_condensing_results_r.html) · [Dorico condensing considerations](https://www.steinberg.help/r/dorico-pro/6.2/en/dorico/topics/page_formatting/page_formatting_condensing_calculations_considerations_c.html)

## VF5 delivery and VF6 decisions

| Decision to record under requirements/ | Evidence that decides it |
| --- | --- |
| Which actions need their own IDs, offsets or string targets? | Select one rasgueado stroke, one bent string in a chord and one simultaneous golpe. Preserve the relationship to the parent attack in staff, TAB and accessible reading order. |
| How should exact score time, a bounded time range and an ensemble cue coexist? | Represent the metered beam, independent bars and free repeat cell. Moving a layout break must preserve a known duration and must never invent an exact onset for a free event. |
| Which decisions belong to the score and which to a layout view? | Compare a condensed score, two independent parts and a transposed cue. Map every displayed event back to its source players. Preserve authored annotations after changing the view. |
