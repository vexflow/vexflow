## Changes and compatibility

| Gap | Behavior on the branch | Acceptance evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| VF-006, VF-012 · Heads and voices | Three altered Ds now occupy three head positions. The accidentals retain their head indices. Ledger lines and horizontal reservations cover the displaced heads. Four or more visible voices use separate columns where their vertical ranges overlap. Hidden voices are excluded. | Whole, half, filled and flagged chords, both stem directions, dots and ledger high Cs. The two-spelling controls match main. A four-voice passage includes a rest, a dot, a sharp and an intervening eighth. Reversing the voices gives the same tested positions. |
| VF-007, VF-010 · Grace groups | In key-aware composition, `graceAccidentals: 'ordered'` processes before-graces, the simultaneous hosts, then after-graces. Before-groups align their final attack and after-groups align their first attack for accidental state. Opposing groups reserve space on their own side of the host. | Expected sign arrays for unequal grace counts, contradictory simultaneous pitches, reversed voice insertion and an authored cautionary. Two staves exercise slashed and beamed grace groups at a system start. |
| VF-004 · Spacing | `new Formatter({ spacing: { mode: 'optical' } })` reserves measured widths on each stave. It distributes spare space in proportion to the square root of the interval to the next onset, including the final duration. | A grace cluster, inline clef, chord symbol, lyric-width annotation and double sharp fit at the calculated minimum. One pixel less gives `InsufficientOpticalSpace` with the required width. An ending in two 64ths fits at a narrower minimum than proportional spacing. |
| VF-005 · Beams | Set `beam.renderOptions.contourSlope = true` before formatting to allow a level beam or a slope following the outer stem tips. A level beam inside the staff snaps its outer edge outward to a half-space. Explicit flat beams and one-sided slope bounds retain their controls. | For the G–A–B–C contour at 300 and 520 px the main slopes are +0.100 and −0.075 in screen coordinates. With the option they are +0.100 and zero. Numeric checks cover both stem directions, minimum stems, beamlet overrides and every endpoint at 190, 340 and 790 px. Companion cases check rest grouping and the quarter-triplet followed by an eighth and two sixteenths. |
| VF-009, VF-011 · Tuplets and attachments | Tuplets and ornaments expose positioned bounds before drawing. Tuplets can specify an engraved `displayRatio` independently of played time. Brackets account for attached articulation and ornament ink. A delayed ornament follows its next attack on reflow. Redrawing a long fall preserves its offset. | Exact fractional onsets and total for a 3:2 group nested inside 5:4. SVG displays 6:4 over a played 3:2 group. Both rendering backends exercise an ornament with upper and lower signs, a slashed grace note, an articulation and a bracket. |

VF5 keeps its existing formatter, beam fitting and explicit grace accidental defaults. Native callers that relied on coincident third heads, ignored fourth voices or accumulated ornament offsets will see different output. Existing applications can enable the new policies independently. Tie continuations and courtesy signs at breaks belong to M4. Phrase-wide stacking and cross-tick routing belong to M3.

Four image differences against main belong to M2. They are `unison-triple`, `m2-four-voices`, `m2-contour` and `m2-ornament-redraw`. The other two are the approved M1 beams. The bounding geometry of `ornament-1` and `ornament-2` also changes with auxiliary sign measurement. Their pixels match main. The historical comparison includes the five M0 fixes already on main.

The browser matrix uses the installed Bravura/Academico and Petaluma/Petaluma Script pairs. Leland runs without supplied SMuFL metadata to exercise fallback. Each pair runs through SVG and Canvas with ordinary, 75% cue and native grace sizes. Tests check drawn content, finite pre-draw geometry, repeatability, head positions, accidental and dot clearance, ledger coverage and stem attachment in the selected examples. [Font matrix](../../tests/node/local-engraving-tests.mjs) · [Placement tests](../../tests/node/engraving-layout-tests.mjs) · [Timing and spacing tests](../../tests/node/proportional-spacing-tests.mjs) · [Accidental tests](../../tests/node/easyscore-tests.mjs)

npx grunt test:regressions qunit
node tools/engraving-baseline.mjs build/m0-release/package/build/cjs/vexflow-debug-with-tests.js
